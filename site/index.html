Which AI should translate your web novel?

48 AI models translated the same eleven chapters of a Korean murim novel into English. Here is how good each one was, and what a chapter costs.

For Korean web novels, Claude Opus 5.5 scored highest in this test, at $1.288 a chapter. Muse Spark 1.2 came within 9 points for $0.0022 a chapter: $0.22 per hundred chapters.

Questions

What is the best AI for translating Korean web novels?

In this benchmark, Claude Opus 5.5 scored highest (86.4 of 100) translating eleven chapters of Return of the Mount Hua Sect (화산귀환) from Korean to English, averaging two AI judges. Claude Sonnet 5.5 (83.5), GPT-6 Astra (83.4), kimi-k3.1-pro (83.1) follow. In blind side-by-side comparisons the order differs: GPT-6 Astra rated highest, so treat the top few as a close group.

What is the cheapest AI that translates web novels well?

Muse Spark 1.2 scored 76.9 at $0.0022 per chapter, about $0.22 for 100 chapters at list price. No model scored higher for less. The chart's labelled models are the best available at each price.

Is Claude or ChatGPT better for translating web novels?

Best Claude model tested: Claude Opus 5.5 (86.4). Best OpenAI model: GPT-6 Astra (83.4). Best Google model: Gemini 3.8 Flash (82.9). Gemini 4 Argon has not been tested yet.

How much does it cost to translate a web novel chapter with AI?

From $0.0013 to $1.288 per chapter of roughly 7,000 Korean characters at list price, depending on the model. Reasoning models spend most of that on thinking tokens before they write: compare the output-token column. Subscriptions change what you pay, not how many tokens a model uses.

How are the translations scored?

Two AI judges, Claude Haiku 5.5 and Gemini 3.8 Flash, each score every chapter from 1 to 5 on six qualities (below) and compare translations two at a time, in both orders, blind to which model wrote them (1610 comparisons pooled). The quality score is their average. Every model gets the same prompt, the same chapters and its maximum reasoning setting.

What the score measures

Accuracy
meaning carried over correctly
Terminology
names, titles and martial-arts terms kept consistent across chapters
Register
tone and speech levels (honorifics, banter, formality)
Fluency
reads as natural English prose
Fidelity
nothing added, dropped or summarised
Sanitization
violence and crude language kept, not softened

Read it with these limits

  • One novel, one language pair: eleven chapters of 화산귀환 (Return of the Mount Hua Sect), Korean to English. Chinese and Japanese works are next.
  • The judges are AI models, and judges can favour their own family. Claude Haiku 5.5 and Gemini 3.8 Flash are judges and are also ranked; compare their score from the other judge in the table. The two judges' scores correlate at 0.98, but Gemini spreads them wider (11 to 90, against 31 to 83 for Claude Haiku 5.5).
  • The ordering is close at the top. The side-by-side comparisons rank the leading models differently from the quality score, and their rating ranges overlap.
  • Claude and GPT models ran through their command-line tools with every tool switched off, Gemini through Google's Antigravity tool, and the rest through their providers' APIs. The text sent was the same.
  • Models change under a stable name. These are the versions tested in run 2026-09, reported 2026-10-28.
